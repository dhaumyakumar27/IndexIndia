The Open-Source Investor: The Master Forensic Evaluation Directory

Abstract: Venture capital and capital allocation in India are plagued by narrative-driven investing. Institutional truth is not found in pitch decks, startup PR announcements, or SaaS growth metrics. Institutional truth is buried in statutory filings, tax disclosures, and cash flow reconciliations. This master directory provides the exact methodologies, public data sources, and forensic frameworks required to strip away the marketing layer and evaluate the true legal, structural, and financial health of any Indian commercial entity.

Module 1: The Architecture of Public Data in India

[ACT I • NODES 01-04]
01.1

📜 Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA V3 Portal)

[MODULE 1 // STATUTORY FILINGS] #sec-01-1

You do not need a ₹20 Lakh/year proprietary data terminal to access institutional truth. The Indian regulatory state mandates massive, transparent, and machine-readable disclosures under the Companies Act, 2013. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA V3) is the foundational source of legal record for every Private Limited (Pvt. Ltd.) and Public Limited (Ltd.) company incorporated in India.

By utilizing the statutory "View Public Documents" facility (costing ₹100 per company for a 3-hour inspection window), an analyst extracts the unvarnished corporate DNA:

  • Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements): Contains the statutory audited balance sheet, profit & loss statement, cash flow statement, and the complete Independent Auditor's Report including all qualified opinions and matter-of-emphasis notes.
  • Form MGT-7 (Annual Return): The ultimate legally binding capitalization table (cap table). It lists every single legal shareholder, voting percentages, director appointments, transfers executed during the fiscal year, and promoter holding dilution.
  • Form CHG-1 & CHG-4 (Creation & Satisfaction of Charges): A "Charge" is a registered legal lien against corporate assets. If a founder claims to be "100% bootstrapped and debt-free," but CHG-1 records a ₹50 Crore hypothecation lien held by an NBFC over intellectual property and book debts, the founder's pitch deck is instantly contradicted by public statute.
  • Form PAS-3 (Return of Allotment): Mandated every time fresh shares or convertible securities are allotted. Lists exact post-money share prices, face value vs. securities premium, and the exact names of institutional subscribers.
PORTAL: MCA V3 STATUTORY FEE: ₹100 KEY FORMS: AOC-4, MGT-7, CHG-1, PAS-3
01.2

📈 BSE / NSE Corporate Filings (For Listed Entities)

[MODULE 1 // EXCHANGE DISCLOSURES] #sec-01-2

Never evaluate an enterprise by downloading glossy, agency-designed PDF Annual Reports. Marketing departments engineer these documents with stylized infographics specifically to draw human eyes away from footnotes, contingent dispute annexures, and related-party schedules.

  • XBRL (eXtensible Business Reporting Language): Always download raw XBRL XML filings directly from the exchange repository. XBRL provides standardized, machine-readable financial tags. You can ingest these directly into automated Python parsing pipelines to calculate gross margins, inventory turns, and working capital cycles with zero Optical Character Recognition (OCR) hallucination risk.
  • Quarterly Earnings Transcripts: Ignore executive scripted monologues. Fast-forward immediately to the institutional analyst Q&A section. When sell-side forensic analysts cross-examine management on why Capital Expenditure (CapEx) timelines are slipping or why trade receivables are ballooning past 120 days, management's spontaneous deflections reveal real operational friction.
REPOSITORIES: BSEINDIA / NSEINDIA DATA FORMAT: RAW XBRL/XML CORE ARTIFACT: ANALYST Q&A TRANSCRIPTS
01.3

📖 SEBI Public Issues Database (The DRHP Prospectus)

[MODULE 1 // CAPITAL MARKETS] #sec-01-3

For late-stage unlisted companies preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO), the Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filed with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the single most valuable forensic document in Indian commerce. Under statutory threat of criminal liability for misstatement, merchant bankers and auditors strip away vanity metrics.

  • 5-Year Restated Financial Statements: Eliminates accounting gimmicks introduced during private fundraising rounds to provide uniform, audited restatements across historical cycles.
  • Litigation & Contingent Tax Annexures: Discloses every active civil dispute, promoter tax assessment order, GST investigation, and criminal proceedings against directors and subsidiary vehicles.
  • Comprehensive Related Party Transactions (RPTs): Forces comprehensive 5-year disclosures of all payments, leases, royalties, and consulting retainers paid to promoter families or affiliated offshore shell entities.
REGULATOR: SEBI DOCUMENT: DRHP / RHP MANDATE: 5-YEAR UNIFORM RESTATED AUDIT
01.4

🗂️ GeM & CPPP Public Procurement Verification

[MODULE 1 // PUBLIC PROCUREMENT] #sec-01-4

For B2B, industrial, and deep-tech enterprises that claim massive sovereign and public sector enterprise traction in their pitch materials, cross-check their claims against statutory public procurement portals.

  • Government e-Marketplace (GeM): Verify registered OEM status, secondary vendor classifications, order fulfillment track records, and actual statutory transaction volume.
  • Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP): Trace whether a startup's publicized "defense or public enterprise award" represents an actual executed contract with a sanctioned purchase order, or merely an Expression of Interest (EOI) participation or preliminary pre-bid conference attendance.
PORTALS: GEM.GOV.IN / EPROCURE.GOV.IN FORENSIC GOAL: VERIFY ACTUAL PURCHASE ORDERS

Module 2: The Forensic Audit Playbook (Red Flag Matrix)

[ACT II • NODES 05-07]
02.1

🔍 The CARO 2020 Annexure (Companies Auditor’s Report Order)

[MODULE 2 // STATUTORY AUDIT] #sec-02-1

A forensic audit is not about calculating compound annual growth rates (CAGR); it is about discovering structural vulnerabilities buried in statutory annexures. Under the Companies Act, statutory auditors must append the CARO 2020 Annexure to every audit report. This order legally obligates auditors to comment on specific corporate pathologies:

  • Clause (iii) — Promoter Loans & Advances: Has the enterprise granted unsecured loans, guarantees, or advances to directors, sister concerns, or founder-controlled entities? This is the primary mechanism through which operating cash is siphoned away into private family accounts.
  • Clause (vii) — Statutory Defaults (PF, ESI, GST): Is the enterprise defaulting on statutory deposits such as Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), or Goods and Services Tax (GST)? An enterprise that cannot deposit employee retirement funds on time is typically within 90 days of an acute working capital seizure.
  • Clause (ix) — End-Use of Borrowings: Were long-term project loans diverted to cover immediate operational payroll or short-term vendor payables? Diverting CapEx term loans into OpEx debt coverage is the hallmark of an imminent Ponzi debt cycle.
  • Clause (xi) — Fraud Reporting: Details any suspected fraud by or against the company reported under Section 143(12) to the Central Government during the financial year.
STATUTE: CARO 2020 HIGH-RISK CLAUSES: (iii) LOANS, (vii) DEFAULTS, (ix) DIVERSION
02.2

📋 Ind AS Notes to Accounts (Accounting Standards Forensics)

[MODULE 2 // ACCOUNTING STANDARDS] #sec-02-2

P&L topline figures are accounting approximations; the truth resides in the Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) disclosure footnotes.

  • Ind AS 24 (Related Party Disclosures): Inspect "Purchases from Related Parties." If a public or venture-backed entity purchases raw materials or consulting services at inflated prices from private LLPs owned by relatives of the promoters, operating cash is diverted while the core company's margins compress.
  • Ind AS 115 (Revenue Recognition & Unbilled Assets): Beware of significant line items labeled "Unbilled Revenue" or "Contract Assets." This reflects revenue recognized upfront on the P&L before the client has been formally billed or accepted delivery, artificially puffing growth multiples.
  • Ind AS 38 (Intangible Assets & R&D Capitalization): Tech enterprises frequently conceal massive operating burn by capitalizing engineer payroll and cloud infrastructure costs as intangible assets on the balance sheet instead of expensing them on the P&L. If these assets fail to yield direct commercial cash flows, they represent phantom balances subject to sudden write-offs.
STANDARDS: IND AS 24, 115, 38 FOCUS: RELATED PARTY TRANSFERS & CAPITALIZED BURN
02.3

⚠️ Contingent Liabilities (The Off-Balance-Sheet Bombs)

[MODULE 2 // OFF-BALANCE-SHEET RISK] #sec-02-3

Contingent liabilities represent potential financial obligations contingent on uncertain judicial or regulatory outcomes. They do not appear on the face of the balance sheet but are listed in statutory notes.

  • Disputed Tax Assessments: Scrutinize pending Income Tax appeals (ITAT/High Court) and GST demand notices. A single adverse ruling can wipe out multiple years of accumulated net worth.
  • Corporate Cross-Guarantees: Examine corporate guarantees provided for debts incurred by subsidiary or affiliate entities. If an affiliate defaults on credit facilities, the parent enterprise becomes immediately liable to bank recovery enforcement under SARFAESI or IBC proceedings.
RADAR: OFF-BALANCE OBLIGATIONS IMPACT: SUDDEN SOLVENCY SEIZURE

Module 3: Capital Structure & Liquidity Stress Testing

[ACT III • NODES 08-10]
03.1

💲 The Cash Conversion Trap (CFO vs. EBITDA Divergence)

[MODULE 3 // CASH FLOW FORENSICS] #sec-03-1

Accounting profit is an estimate; operating cash flow is an empirical reality. Stress test the entity's circulatory health by benchmarking Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) against EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization).

  • The Cardinal Rule: If an enterprise regularly reports ₹100 Crore in EBITDA, but statutory CFO over the same multi-year period averages only ₹10 Crore, the reported profit is fictitious. It is trapped in uncollected receivables, channel-stuffed distributor inventory, or fabricated paper billings.
  • Channel Stuffing Diagnostics: Rapid surge in Q4 trade receivables accompanied by expanding inventory days is a classic indicator that goods were pushed to distributors without genuine end-consumer demand.
FORMULA: CFO / EBITDA RATIO BENCHMARK: RATIO > 0.70 WARNING: DIVERGENCE > 3 CONSECUTIVE YEARS
03.2

💩 The Promoter Pledge Death Spiral

[MODULE 3 // PROMOTER RISK] #sec-03-2

In listed and pre-IPO enterprises, examine exchange shareholding disclosures for promoter encumbrance patterns:

  • Mechanics of the Spiral: Promoters frequently pledge their equity shares to financial institutions as collateral for personal borrowing or unlisted real-estate ventures. If the enterprise's market capitalization drops, lenders issue immediate margin calls.
  • Cascading Liquidation: If the promoter cannot deposit cash collateral within 24 to 48 hours, institutional lenders invoke the pledge and dump shares on the open exchange, sparking an unstoppable downward liquidation cascade. Any promoter pledge exceeding 15% constitutes an unacceptable systemic risk.
THRESHOLD: >15% PLEDGE = HIGH RISK TRIGGER: MARGIN CALL FORCED OPEN MARKET SELLING
03.3

⏱ The Working Capital Cycle Mismatch

[MODULE 3 // WORKING CAPITAL] #sec-03-3

Hyper-growth in an enterprise operating under a structurally negative working capital cycle accelerates bankruptcy rather than enterprise value.

  • Receivable Days (DSO): The number of days clients take to settle invoices (e.g., 90 to 120 days in Indian enterprise B2B).
  • Payable Days (DPO): The payment terms demanded by critical suppliers (e.g., 30 to 45 days).
  • The Cash Deficit Gap: If clients settle invoices in 90 days but suppliers require cash in 45 days, the company has an unhedged 45-day structural cash hole. Every incremental ₹10 Crore in top-line sales widens this cash deficit, forcing the company to take on punitive short-term debt just to sustain baseline delivery.
EQUATION: CASH CONVERSION CYCLE (CCC) = DSO + DIO - DPO DEFICIT SQUEEZE: DSO >> DPO

Module 4: The Deep-Tech & Industrial Overlay

[ACT IV • NODES 11-13]
04.1

⚙️ NRE vs. Burn Rate Architecture

[MODULE 4 // INDUSTRIAL ECONOMICS] #sec-04-1

SaaS heuristics (CAC, LTV, Net Retention) are inapplicable and actively dangerous when applied to hardware, aerospace, robotics, precision manufacturing, or industrial deep-tech.

  • The SaaS Flaw: SaaS builds software upfront via equity burn, hoping to amortize customer acquisition costs across recurring subscriptions.
  • The Deep-Tech Framework: Industrial hardware companies cannot survive purely on speculative venture burn. Look for Non-Recurring Engineering (NRE) revenue. Did an industrial conglomerate (e.g., L&T, Tata, Bharat Forge) pay the startup an upfront NRE milestone fee to design and customize the automated robotics prototype for factory testing? Upfront NRE confirms genuine enterprise demand and funds physical prototyping without equity dilution.
MODEL: NRE (NON-RECURRING ENGINEERING) VALIDATION: UPFRONT CONGLOMERATE COMMITMENT
04.2

🚀 Sovereign Technology Readiness Level (TRL 1-9)

[MODULE 4 // MATURITY BENCHMARK] #sec-04-2

Do not finance academic science experiments disguised as commercial deep-tech startups. Map intellectual property against the standardized Technology Readiness Level (TRL 1-9) scale:

  • TRL 1–3 (Basic Principles & Lab Concept): Academic research, university bench prototypes, zero commercial repeatability. Should be backed by government grants (BIRAC, DST, iDEX), not venture capital equity.
  • TRL 4–5 (Validation in Simulated Environment): The hazardous "Valley of Death." Components operate in controlled conditions but have not survived factory-floor industrial tolerances.
  • TRL 6–7 (System Prototype in Operational Environment): Full engineering prototype validated under actual industrial client operating conditions. This is the exact milestone where institutional growth capital belongs.
  • TRL 8–9 (System Qualified & Mission Proven): Certified hardware executing in full-rate series production.
SCALE: TRL 1 TO TRL 9 VC SWEET SPOT: TRL 6-7 TRANSITION
04.3

🛡️ Homologation and Regulatory Certification Moats

[MODULE 4 // REGULATORY MOATS] #sec-04-3

Patents provide negligible protection in India because patent litigation in commercial courts frequently drags on for over a decade. The authentic, defensible moats in deep-tech are statutory homologation and sovereign regulatory clearances:

  • Automotive & EV: Has the propulsion and battery management architecture achieved formal ARAI (Automotive Research Association of India) or ICAT homologation?
  • Aerospace & Drones: Does the payload and airframe hold formal DGCA Type Certification?
  • Defense & Avionics: Has the hardware obtained CEMILAC (Centre for Military Airworthiness and Certification) or DGAQA clearance?
  • The Moat Reality: A well-funded competitor can attempt to reverse-engineer software in months; they cannot bypass 24 months of rigorous statutory homologation and crash safety testing. Statutory certification is the moat.
SOVEREIGN BODIES: ARAI, DGCA, CEMILAC, ICAT BARRIER TO ENTRY: 18-36 MONTH CERTIFICATION TESTING

Synthesis & Due Diligence Checklist

[ACT V • NODES 14-15]
05.1

✅ Master Forensic Due Diligence Checklist

[SYNTHESIS // PRE-INVESTMENT RADAR] #sec-05-1

Before wiring capital or syndicating investment memos on any Indian enterprise, complete this 10-point statutory forensic checklist:

  1. MCA Verification: Pull AOC-4, MGT-7, and CHG-1 for the trailing 3 fiscal years. Verify all registered charges and confirm equity capitalization.
  2. Auditor Tenure & Changes: Has the statutory auditor resigned mid-term without completing the audit cycle? A mid-term auditor resignation is a code-red signal of irreconcilable fraud disputes.
  3. CARO Default Audit: Review CARO 2020 Clauses (iii), (vii), (ix), and (xi) for promoter loans, statutory PF/TDS payment defaults, or borrowing diversions.
  4. CFO vs EBITDA Quality Check: Verify that multi-year Cash Flow from Operations equals at least 70% of reported EBITDA.
  5. Related Party Transfer Audit: Cross-check Ind AS 24 disclosures to ensure revenues and profits are not circulating through founder-owned LLPs.
  6. Revenue Integrity: Review Ind AS 115 unbilled revenue notes to guarantee bills reflect completed deliverables with legal collection recourse.
  7. Capitalized Burn Scrutiny: Audit Ind AS 38 intangible assets to verify salary expenses are not being parked on the balance sheet.
  8. Contingent Tax Scan: Tabulate all active Income Tax and GST show-cause demand notices listed in disclosure notes.
  9. Promoter Encumbrance: Confirm promoter equity pledges on BSE/NSE stay safely below 15% of total promoter holding.
  10. Sovereign Homologation (For Deep-Tech): Demand verified ARAI, DGCA, or CEMILAC clearance certificates before modeling unit economics.
FRAMEWORK: 10-POINT FORENSIC RADAR VERDICT: PASS ONLY UPON 100% RECONCILIATION

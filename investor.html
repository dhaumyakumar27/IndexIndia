Module 1: The Architecture of Public Data in India

[ACT I • 10 NODES]
├── 📂 01.1

📜 Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA V3 Portal)

[BRANCH • 4 SUB-NODES]
[MODULE 1 // STATUTORY FILINGS] #sec-01-1

You do not need a ₹20 Lakh/year proprietary data terminal to access institutional truth. The Indian regulatory state mandates massive, transparent, and machine-readable disclosures under the Companies Act, 2013. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA V3) is the foundational source of legal record for every Private Limited (Pvt. Ltd.) and Public Limited (Ltd.) company incorporated in India.

By utilizing the statutory "View Public Documents" facility (costing ₹100 per company for a 3-hour inspection window), an analyst extracts the unvarnished corporate DNA across four mandatory statutory disclosures:

PORTAL: MCA V3 STATUTORY FEE: ₹100 NODES: 01.1.1 (AOC-4) • 01.1.2 (MGT-7) • 01.1.3 (CHG-1) • 01.1.4 (PAS-3)
│  ├── 📄 01.1.1

📜 Form AOC-4: Balance Sheet Forensics & Statutory Audit Notes

[LEAF • 01.1.1]
[MCA V3 // FINANCIAL STATEMENTS // 1.1.1] #sec-01-1-1

Mandated under Section 137 of the Companies Act, 2013, Form AOC-4 (or AOC-4 XBRL for companies with paid-up capital ≥ ₹5 Crore or turnover ≥ ₹100 Crore) is filed annually within 30 days of the Annual General Meeting (AGM). It contains the complete audited financial statements (Standalone and Consolidated), the Independent Auditor's Report, Directors' Report, and the CARO annexure. This is the ultimate ground-truth repository that renders founder pitch decks redundant.

  • The Auditor Opinion Spectrum: Look past the top-line numbers directly at the Independent Auditor's Report. Categorize the opinion immediately:
    • Unqualified ("Clean") Opinion: The auditor certifies that the accounts present a true and fair view in accordance with statutory accounting standards.
    • Qualified Opinion: The auditor expresses reservations regarding specific accounting treatments—such as unverified inventory physical counts, disputed debtor balances, or inadequate provisioning for doubtful debts.
    • Adverse Opinion: The auditor concludes that misstatements are both material and pervasive, rendering the financial statements misleading.
    • Disclaimer of Opinion: The auditor states that they were unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence to form an opinion—an immediate code-red warning of operational obstruction or systemic fraud.
  • Deciphering "Emphasis of Matter" & Going Concern (SA 570 / Ind AS 1): Auditors frequently use "Emphasis of Matter" paragraphs to highlight existential business risks without formally qualifying their opinion. Look for disclosures regarding massive pending GST/Income Tax demand notices, insolvency petitions filed by operational creditors at NCLT, or accumulated losses eroding 100% of the entity's net worth.
  • Auditor Quality & Peer Review Verification: Verify the identity, ICAI registration number, and tenure of the signing Chartered Accountant. If a venture-funded unicorn or high-growth manufacturing entity with hundreds of crores in turnover employs a sole-proprietor audit firm with no ICAI Peer Review Certificate or National Financial Reporting Authority (NFRA) oversight, internal financial controls are virtually unmonitored.
STATUTORY FILING: FORM AOC-4 / AOC-4 XBRL GOVERNING SECTION: CA 2013 SEC 137 CRITICAL INSPECTION: INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT & EMPHASIS OF MATTER
│  ├── 📄 01.1.2

📊 Form MGT-7: Cap Table Verification & Beneficial Ownership (SBO Rule 9)

[LEAF • 01.1.2]
[MCA V3 // CAPITAL STRUCTURE // 1.1.2] #sec-01-1-2

Filed under Section 92 within 60 days of the AGM, Form MGT-7 (Annual Return) is the company's statutory snapshot of legal ownership and management. It is the only legally admissible cap table in the Republic of India as of March 31 of each financial year, overriding all informal investor spreadsheets.

  • Statutory Cap Table & Transfer Ledgers: Form MGT-7 itemizes the complete register of members: equity share counts, Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS), Optional Convertible Preference Shares (OCPS), differential voting rights, and exact nominal face values. Crucially, it lists all share transfers executed during the fiscal year—revealing undisclosed founder secondaries, angel exits, or promoter stake liquidations executed quietly behind the scenes.
  • Significant Beneficial Ownership (SBO) Forensics (Section 90): The Ministry of Corporate Affairs mandates disclosure of natural persons who hold indirect ownership (>10%) or exercise significant influence or control under the Companies (Significant Beneficial Owners) Rules, 2018 (Form BEN-2). This forensic trail pierces shell corporate veils, unmasking promoter control routed through Mauritius SPVs, Singapore holding entities, or domestic private LLPs owned by family relatives.
  • Promoter Dilution Red Flags: Compare consecutive MGT-7 filings across three trailing fiscal years. A sudden drop in founder equity shareholding percentage preceding a capital raise—unexplained by fresh external primary issuance—indicates that founders are de-risking their personal exposure while pitching hyper-growth to new institutional LPs.
STATUTORY FILING: FORM MGT-7 / MGT-7A STATUTE: CA 2013 SEC 90 & 92 KEY ASSET: BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP & SHARE TRANSFER LEDGER
│  ├── 📄 01.1.3

🔗 Form CHG-1 & CHG-4: Asset Hypothecation & Hidden Debt Liens

[LEAF • 01.1.3]
[MCA V3 // REGISTER OF CHARGES // 1.1.3] #sec-01-1-3

Under Section 77 of the Companies Act, 2013, every company creating a legal charge on its assets, property, or undertakings must register the instrument with the Registrar of Companies (RoC) within 30 days using Form CHG-1. When the debt is fully repaid, Form CHG-4 must be filed to record satisfaction of the charge.

  • Unmasking Hidden Leverage & Hypothecations: Startup executives frequently claim in investor meetings to be "completely debt-free and equity-funded." Querying the MCA Register of Charges exposes reality in seconds. If active CHG-1 filings reveal hypothecation over current assets, intellectual property, book debts, or plant & machinery in favor of Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) or venture debt funds, the entity is burdened with fixed financial servicing obligations.
  • IBC Liquidation Seniority Waterfall: In any insolvency or corporate distress event under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC, 2016), registered charge holders are categorized as Secured Financial Creditors. They hold first-priority legal claims on asset recovery under Section 53. Unsecured convertible noteholders (CCPS/CCD) and common equity shareholders rank near the absolute bottom of the payout waterfall.
  • Charge Modification Spikes as Distress Signals: Examine the chronological history of charge modifications. Consecutive filings of Form CHG-1 for charge modification—expanding pledged collateral to cover additional overdraft facilities or extending repayment dates—indicate that commercial banks are tightening covenants and demanding additional collateral coverage to counter deteriorating operating liquidity.
STATUTORY FORMS: CHG-1 (CREATION) & CHG-4 (SATISFACTION) STATUTE: CA 2013 SEC 77-87 WATERFALL IMPACT: SECURED FINANCIAL CREDITOR PRIORITY
│  └── 📄 01.1.4

💸 Form PAS-3: Private Round Share Pricing & Premium Discrepancies

[LEAF • 01.1.4]
[MCA V3 // RETURN OF ALLOTMENT // 1.1.4] #sec-01-1-4

Whenever a company issues fresh shares or convertible securities under a private placement, preferential allotment, or rights issue, it is legally obligated under Section 39 and Section 42 of the Companies Act, 2013 to submit Form PAS-3 (Return of Allotment) within 15–30 days of allotment.

  • Empirical Valuation Reconstruction: Pitch deck valuation multiples are PR artifacts. Form PAS-3 mandates exact statutory financial accounting: the nominal face value per security (e.g., ₹10 or ₹100), the exact Securities Premium per share paid in cash (e.g., ₹8,420), total consideration mobilized, and the exact count of allotted securities. By multiplying total post-issuance diluted shares by the PAS-3 allotment price, an analyst establishes the true empirical post-money benchmark.
  • Verifying Institutional Subscriber Authenticity: Form PAS-3 includes a mandatory annexure listing the full legal names, Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), passport identifiers, and exact allotment shares for every subscriber. This statutory list prevents founders from claiming participation by tier-1 global venture funds when the round was actually bridged by domestic relatives or high-net-worth friends at heavily discounted terms.
  • Valuation Math & Section 56(2)(viib) Scrutiny: Companies issuing shares at substantial premiums must file an accompanying Valuation Report prepared by an IBBI Registered Valuer or SEBI Category-I Merchant Banker under Rule 11UA of the Income Tax Rules. Compare the discounted cash flow (DCF) projections in the valuation report against the historical financials reported in AOC-4. If the valuer assumed 150% CAGR revenue expansion for five years to justify the premium, the company faces severe tax reassessment scrutiny under Section 56(2)(viib).
STATUTORY FILING: FORM PAS-3 STATUTE: CA 2013 SEC 39 & 42 VALUATION BENCHMARK: SECURITIES PREMIUM PER SHARE & RULE 11UA DCF

💻 Machine-Readable XBRL Pipelines

[LEAF • 01.2.1]
[EXCHANGE // DATA ARCHITECTURE // 1.2.1] #sec-01-2-1

Always download raw XBRL (eXtensible Business Reporting Language) XML filings directly from BSE India or NSE India repositories. XBRL provides standardized, machine-readable financial tags mandated by SEBI under Regulation 33 of LODR.

  • Zero-OCR Parsing Pipelines: Ingesting raw XBRL feeds directly into automated Python parsing engines guarantees error-free metric extraction (gross margins, inventory turns, days sales outstanding) without Optical Character Recognition (OCR) hallucination risks.
  • Segment Reporting Disaggregation: In the glossy annual report, management frequently groups loss-making experimental subsidiaries into a broad "Others" segment. XBRL schedules force granular taxonomy tagging, uncovering operating losses in peripheral ventures.
REPOSITORIES: BSEINDIA / NSEINDIA FORMAT: RAW XBRL/XML REGULATION: SEBI LODR REG 33
│  └── 📄 01.2.2

🎩 Earnings Call Analyst Q&A Cross-Examination

[LEAF • 01.2.2]
[EXCHANGE // QUALITATIVE FORENSICS // 1.2.2] #sec-01-2-2

Executive prepared remarks during quarterly earnings calls are polished by investor relations firms. Fast-forward immediately to the institutional analyst Q&A section to detect operational vulnerability.

  • Cross-Examination Under Fire: When sell-side forensic analysts cross-examine management on why Capital Expenditure (CapEx) commissioning timelines are slipping or why trade receivables are ballooning past 120 days, management's spontaneous deflections reveal real operational friction.
  • Deflection Heuristics: Track rhetorical evasions such as "we will take this offline," sudden CFO turnover announcements on a Friday evening, or changes in guidance terminology (e.g., shifting from "EBITDA margin" to "Contribution margin before central allocations").
ARTIFACT: UNEDITED AUDIO & TRANSCRIPTS FOCUS: ANALYST Q&A CROSS-EXAMINATION

📖 SEBI Public Issues Database (The DRHP Prospectus)

[BRANCH • 2 SUB-NODES]
[MODULE 1 // CAPITAL MARKETS] #sec-01-3

For late-stage unlisted companies preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO), the Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filed with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the single most valuable forensic document in Indian commerce. Under statutory threat of criminal liability for misstatement under Section 34 & 35 of the Companies Act, merchant bankers and auditors strip away vanity metrics.

REGULATOR: SEBI DOCUMENT: DRHP / RHP NODES: 01.3.1 (RESTATED AUDIT) • 01.3.2 (LITIGATION ANNEXURES)
│  ├── 📄 01.3.1

📋 5-Year Restated Financial Audits

[LEAF • 01.3.1]
[SEBI // RESTATED AUDIT // 1.3.1] #sec-01-3-1

During private fundraising cycles, startups frequently modify depreciation schedules, change revenue recognition benchmarks, or capitalize software development expenses to present higher gross margins to venture capital investors.

  • Uniform Historical Accounting: SEBI mandates 5-year uniform restated financial statements audited by independent peer-reviewed accountants. This eliminates historical accounting discrepancies, aligning all prior fiscal periods under uniform Ind AS standards.
  • Adjusted EBITDA Normalization: The DRHP exposes the true statutory gap between "Operating Cash Flow" and "Pro-Forma Adjusted EBITDA," forcing management to reconcile all one-off additions, ESOP expense write-backs, and share-based compensation charges.
MANDATE: 5-YEAR UNIFORM RESTATED AUDIT REGULATOR: SEBI ICDR REGULATIONS
│  └── 📄 01.3.2

⚖️ Litigation & Contingent Tax Demand Annexures

[LEAF • 01.3.2]
[SEBI // LITIGATION RISKS // 1.3.2] #sec-01-3-2

The "Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments" section of the DRHP provides an exhaustive catalogue of every judicial risk threatening the enterprise and its promoters.

  • Direct & Indirect Tax Demands: Details all contested GST assessments, customs classification disputes, and income tax reassessments pending before CIT(A), ITAT, and High Courts. Summing these claims frequently reveals contingent obligations exceeding multiple years of operating profit.
  • Criminal & Regulatory Proceedings: Discloses pending investigations by the Directorate of Enforcement (ED), Serious Fraud Investigation Office (SFIO), Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), or SEBI against promoters, directors, and subsidiary holding entities.
STATUTE: SEBI ICDR SCHEDULE VI RADAR: TAX SHOW-CAUSE & CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS

🏢 GeM Portal OEM Classification & True B2G Fulfillment

[LEAF • 01.4.1]
[PROCUREMENT // GEM PORTAL // 1.4.1] #sec-01-4-1

On the Government e-Marketplace (GeM), verify whether the entity is registered as an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) or merely an uncertified reseller.

  • Order Fulfillment Track Record: Search the vendor's transaction history to verify completed contract values, delivery timeliness scores, and incidents of blacklisting or disqualification by central ministries or defense procurement agencies.

📝 CPPP Tender Tracking (Sanctioned POs vs. EOI Bids)

[LEAF • 01.4.2]
[PROCUREMENT // CPPP TENDERS // 1.4.2] #sec-01-4-2

On the Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP), trace whether a company's publicized "defense or public enterprise award" represents an executed commercial purchase order or merely pre-bid participation.

  • Award of Contract (AOC) vs. Bid Submission: Startups regularly issue press releases celebrating their "selection" in public tenders when they have merely submitted a technical bid. Query the CPPP Tender Award database to confirm issuance of the formal Letter of Award (LoA) and signed contract.

Module 2: The Forensic Audit Playbook (Red Flag Matrix)

[ACT II • 10 NODES]
├── 📂 02.1

🔍 The CARO 2020 Annexure (Companies Auditor’s Report Order)

[BRANCH • 4 SUB-NODES]
[MODULE 2 // STATUTORY AUDIT] #sec-02-1

A forensic audit is not about calculating compound annual growth rates (CAGR); it is about discovering structural vulnerabilities buried in statutory annexures. Under the Companies Act, statutory auditors must append the CARO 2020 Annexure to every audit report. This order legally obligates auditors to comment on specific corporate pathologies:

STATUTE: CARO 2020 NODES: 02.1.1 (LOANS) • 02.1.2 (DEFAULTS) • 02.1.3 (DIVERSION) • 02.1.4 (FRAUD)
│  ├── 📄 02.1.1

💰 Clause (iii) — Unsecured Promoter Loans & Siphoning Trails

[LEAF • 02.1.1]
[CARO 2020 // CLAUSE III // 2.1.1] #sec-02-1-1

Clause (iii) legally mandates auditors to report whether the company has made investments in, provided any guarantee or security, or granted any loans or advances in the nature of loans, secured or unsecured, to companies, firms, Limited Liability Partnerships, or any other parties.

  • The Siphoning Pipeline: Check if loans were granted without formal repayment schedules, at zero interest, or subordinated to external lenders. This is the primary mechanism through which operating cash is quietly siphoned away into private family businesses or promoter-held real estate.
  • Evergreening of Overdue Advances: Check sub-clause (e) and (f) disclosures: Has any loan fallen due during the year and been renewed, extended, or settled by fresh loans? Evergreening promoter advances is an open declaration of unrecoverable capital flight.
STATUTE: CARO 2020 CLAUSE 3(iii) RED FLAG: INTEREST-FREE UNSECURED ADVANCES
│  ├── 📄 02.1.2

⚠️ Clause (vii) — Statutory Defaults (PF, ESI, TDS & GST Seizures)

[LEAF • 02.1.2]
[CARO 2020 // CLAUSE VII // 2.1.2] #sec-02-1-2

Clause (vii) obligates the auditor to report whether the company is regular in depositing undisputed statutory dues including Goods and Services Tax (GST), Provident Fund (PF), Employees' State Insurance (ESI), and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS).

  • The 90-Day Seizure Horizon: An enterprise that defaults on employee PF or TDS deposits is typically within 90 days of an acute working capital seizure. Founders will delay statutory payments only when commercial bank accounts and overdraft facilities are completely dry.
  • Unpaid Dues Exceeding 6 Months: Scrutinize the statutory annexure table listing dues outstanding for more than 6 months from the date they became payable. Significant arrears trigger immediate bank account attachments by EPFO or GST authorities.
STATUTE: CARO 2020 CLAUSE 3(vii) HAZARD: 6-MONTH UNDISPOSABLE DUES & ATTACHMENT

🔄 Clause (ix) — CapEx Term Loan Diversion into OpEx Debt

[LEAF • 02.1.3]

Clause (ix) mandates auditors to disclose whether loans raised for a specific long-term project or capital expenditure were diverted to meet short-term operational expenses or pay off other lenders.

  • CapEx-to-OpEx Diversion: Diverting sanctioned factory equipment loans into monthly sales payroll or distributor rebates is the classic diagnostic signature of an enterprise entering an irrecoverable debt spiral.
  • Willful Defaulter Disclosures: Sub-clause (b) legally requires auditors to confirm whether the company or its promoters have been formally declared a "Willful Defaulter" by any bank or financial institution under RBI guidelines.

🚨 Clause (xi) — Fraud Reporting & Section 143(12) Alerts

[LEAF • 02.1.4]
[CARO 2020 // CLAUSE XI // 2.1.4] #sec-02-1-4

Clause (xi) obligates statutory auditors to report any fraud noticed or reported by the company or by its officers during the financial year, as well as whether a formal Form ADT-4 was filed with the Central Government under Section 143(12).

  • Section 143(12) Whistleblower Mandate: If an auditor has reason to believe that an offense involving fraud of ₹1 Crore or more is being committed against the company by its officers or employees, they are mandated by criminal law to report it directly to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs within 45 days.

[LEAF • 02.2.1]
[IND AS 24 // RELATED PARTY // 2.2.1] #sec-02-2-1

Ind AS 24 mandates comprehensive disclosure of all transactions, outstanding balances, and commitments between the reporting entity and its Key Management Personnel (KMP), promoter family members, or enterprises under common control.

[LEAF • 02.2.2]
[IND AS 115 // REVENUE TIMING // 2.2.2] #sec-02-2-2

Under Ind AS 115 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers), revenue can be recognized over time based on the "percentage of completion" method or performance obligation milestones.

  • The "Unbilled Revenue" Mirage: Inspect the balance sheet line item "Contract Assets" or "Unbilled Revenue." When contract assets surge faster than invoiced collections, management is pulling future phantom revenues into current fiscal periods.

[LEAF • 02.2.3]

Under Ind AS 38, research costs must be expensed immediately, but development expenses may be capitalized as "Intangible Assets under Development" if strict technical feasibility criteria are met.

  • Masking Operational Burn: Software and deep-tech startups regularly disguise massive operating losses by routing software engineer salaries, AWS cloud compute, and prototyping expenses to the balance sheet as capital assets instead of operational expenses on the P&L.
  • The Amortization Reality Check: When these capitalized intangibles fail to generate direct, verifiable commercial cash flows within 24 months, statutory auditors force massive, sudden impairment write-downs that immediately plunge EBITDA deep into the red.

📊 The CFO / EBITDA Divergence Ratio Benchmark

[LEAF • 03.1.1]
[CASH FLOW // RATIO BENCHMARK // 3.1.1] #sec-03-1-1

Calculate the multi-year cumulative ratio of statutory Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) to reported EBITDA across trailing 3 to 5 fiscal years:

📦 Channel Stuffing Diagnostics & Receivables Aging

[LEAF • 03.1.2]
[CASH FLOW // CHANNEL STUFFING // 3.1.2] #sec-03-1-2

Channel stuffing is the practice of forcibly pushing excess inventory onto distributors or retail dealers at quarter-end to book premature sales.

  • Q4 Revenue Spike Anomaly: Inspect quarterly revenue cadence. If 40% to 50% of full-year revenue is booked in the final month of the fiscal year (March) with zero corresponding increase in cash collection, inventory was pushed forward with informal buy-back promises.
  • Receivables Aging Schedules: Companies must now disclose aged Trade Receivables schedules in AOC-4 notes (split into <6 months, 6M-1Yr, 1-2 Yrs, 2-3 Yrs, and >3 Yrs). An increasing proportion of receivables aging past 180 days is direct proof of disputed or uncollectible invoices.

⚡ The Margin Call Liquidation Cascade

[LEAF • 03.2.1]
[PROMOTER RISK // PLEDGE CASCADE // 3.2.1] #sec-03-2-1

Promoters pledge equity shares to NBFCs and mutual funds as collateral for Loan Against Shares (LAS) borrowing to fund private real estate, unlisted acquisitions, or lavish lifestyle spending.

  • The Margin Trigger: Institutional loan agreements mandate minimum security cover ratios (typically 1.75x to 2.5x). When broader market sentiment or sector headwinds cause the share price to decline by 15%–20%, the lender issues an immediate cash margin call.
  • The Cascading Crash: If the promoter cannot deposit cash collateral within 24 to 48 hours, institutional lenders invoke the pledge and dump millions of shares on the exchange floor. This forced institutional selling triggers circuit breakers, crashes market cap, and wipes out public minority shareholders.

🕸️ Multi-Tier Holding Companies & LAS Structures

[LEAF • 03.2.2]
[PROMOTER RISK // HOLDING CO DEBT // 3.2.2] #sec-03-2-2

Sophisticated promoters attempt to conceal pledge risks by interposing unlisted holding companies between themselves and the listed operating company.

│  ├── 📄 03.3.1

🕑 Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC = DSO + DIO - DPO)

[LEAF • 03.3.1]
[WORKING CAPITAL // CCC FORMULA // 3.3.1] #sec-03-3-1

Deconstruct operational duration across the three pillars of commercial liquidity:

  • Days Sales Outstanding (DSO): The average time required to collect receivables from corporate clients (90 to 120 days in Indian enterprise B2B).
  • Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO): The number of days raw materials and finished inventory sit in warehouses before shipment (45 to 60 days).
  • Days Payable Outstanding (DPO): The credit window allowed by component vendors and critical suppliers (30 to 45 days).
  • The Cash Conversion Cycle Equation: CCC = DSO + DIO - DPO. If DSO is 110 days, DIO is 50 days, and DPO is 40 days, the company's CCC is 120 days. The company must self-fund 120 days of operating expenses with external cash.
EQUATION: CCC = DSO + DIO - DPO BENCHMARK: SHORTER / NEGATIVE CCC IS SUPERIOR
│  └── 📄 03.3.2

⚠️ The Growth Insolvency Trap

[LEAF • 03.3.2]
[WORKING CAPITAL // GROWTH TRAP // 3.3.2] #sec-03-3-2

In an enterprise with an unhedged working capital deficit (DSO >> DPO), top-line growth is a lethal accelerant:

  • The Structural Cash Hole: If suppliers must be paid in 45 days but enterprise customers settle bills in 120 days, every incremental ₹10 Crore in revenue widens the cash deficit by ₹2.5 Crore.
  • The Liquidity Wall: If external equity funding dries up or bank working capital limits are capped, the company cannot pay component suppliers. Production halts, orders are cancelled, and the company enters IBC insolvency while boasting record order books.
PARADOX: PROFITABLE ON P&L BUT CASH INSOLVENT DANGER PATTERN: REVENUE EXPANSION OUTPACING WORKING CAPITAL LIMITS

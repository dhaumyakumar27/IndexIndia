AI-generated answers, summaries, code, and comparisons are auxiliary reference guides only. They do not constitute formal legal opinions from an Advocate on Record, formal audit opinions from a statutory auditor, or binding administrative rulings from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) or Registrar of Companies (ROC). Always corroborate critical filing codes, statutory due dates, and board quorum rules against official gazette notifications.

RATE LIMITS, CONTEXT QUOTAS & MODEL RETENTION

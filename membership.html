How does my payment connect to my account?

When you log in, your unique Supabase User ID is passed to Razorpay. Upon successful payment verification, your record is written to the user_memberships table. Your status updates immediately across all your devices.

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept all Indian UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Cred, BHIM), Indian & International Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking across 50+ banks, and EMI options via Razorpay.

How do monthly and annual passes work?

All passes are transparent, single-term settlements (30 days for Monthly, 365 days for Annual, or permanent for Lifetime). There are zero surprise auto-debits or recurring locks on your UPI or card. When your term nears expiration, you can renew in 1 click from your Account panel.

The 100-Point Carry Pool: Aligning Active Builders Without Salary Drag

In capital-constrained research operations, traditional market salaries create severe cash burn before product-market validation. To align seasoned operators (supply chain specialists, enterprise B2B rainmakers), equity pools must be structured around direct exit liquidation rights rather than dilutive common voting shares.

By utilizing contractually enforceable Synthetic Carry Points inside the holding company framework, operators earn an uncompromised percentage of the realization events when a specialized subsidiary reaches commercial viability or Series A handoff.

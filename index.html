01. Unincorporated & Direct-Tax Specific Entities

These structures do not possess a separate corporate identity from their creators or are recognized primarily as distinct taxable units ("Persons") under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (erstwhile Section 2(31) of the Income-tax Act, 1961).

Sole Proprietorship

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: No dedicated corporate statute; recognized via localized commercial registrations (MSME Udyam Registration, state-specific Shops & Establishments Acts, or GST).
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: 1 resident Indian individual with a personal PAN card and bank account. Zero minimum capital requirement.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ₹0 for online MSME Udyam registration (~15 minutes); ~₹1,000–₹5,000 and ~2–7 days if adding a local Shop & Establishment license or GST registration.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Unlimited personal liability (personal house and savings are exposed to business creditors). Taxed at individual slab rates; eligible for presumptive taxation (50% deemed profit on gross receipts up to ₹75 Lakhs/year for professionals under erstwhile Section 44ADA / Section 58 of the 2025 Act). Mandatory GST registration triggers only at ₹20 Lakhs turnover for services or ₹40 Lakhs for goods.
  • CONTEXT: Optimal for Day-1 solo researchers, consultants, and freelancers keeping overhead near zero before crossing commercial liability triggers.
  • EXAMPLE: An independent bioinformatics researcher developing a proprietary algorithmic model for protein-folding prediction.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Obtain Udyam (MSME) Registration: Log in to the official portal (udyamregistration.gov.in), authenticate your personal identity and PAN, select your NIC (National Industrial Classification) activity code, enter bank and establishment details, and generate the free e-certificate (~15 minutes).
  2. 2. Secure Local Municipal License (Shop & Establishment): Apply on your state's labor department portal (e.g., ENagar in Gujarat or Aaple Sarkar in Maharashtra) with your rent agreement/NOC and utility bill within 30 days of starting operations.
  3. 3. Register for GST (If Applicable): File Form GST REG-01 on gst.gov.in if your annual turnover crosses ₹20 Lakhs for services or ₹40 Lakhs for goods, or if engaging in inter-state B2B sales.
  4. 4. Open a Current Bank Account: Submit at least two proof-of-business documents (such as the Udyam Certificate + Shop Act license or GST certificate) alongside your personal PAN to open a dedicated current account.

Partnership Firm (Unregistered & Registered)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: The Indian Partnership Act, 1932.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Minimum 2 partners, maximum 50 partners (capped via Section 464 of the Companies Act, 2013). Requires a written Partnership Deed on stamp paper. Zero statutory minimum capital.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹2,000–₹5,000 (1–3 days) for an unregistered notarized deed; ~₹5,000–₹15,000 (7–15 days) to register with the State Registrar of Firms (ROF).
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Unlimited, joint, and several personal liability for all partners. Taxed at a flat 30% rate (plus surcharge/cess) with zero corporate shield. Unregistered firms face a severe statutory disability under Section 69: they cannot file lawsuits against third parties to enforce contracts.
  • CONTEXT: Dangerous for deep-tech, R&D, or capital-intensive ventures due to personal liability exposure; largely replaced by the LLP.
  • EXAMPLE: A boutique industrial design studio researching and prototyping ergonomic CAD blueprints for 3D-printed orthopedic casts.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Draft & Execute the Partnership Deed: Draft the deed defining partner names, capital contributions, profit/loss sharing ratios, and dispute resolution clauses. Print it on non-judicial e-stamp paper of the value prescribed by your state's Stamp Act (~₹500 to ₹5,000+), have all partners and two witnesses sign, and notarize it. (An unregistered firm stops after obtaining its PAN and bank account, though it remains barred from suing third parties on contracts under Section 69).
  2. 2. Apply for a Firm PAN & TAN: Submit Form 49A (PAN) and Form 49B (TAN) via Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL portals using the notarized Partnership Deed as proof of existence.
  3. 3. Register with the State Registrar of Firms (ROF): File Form I on your state's ROF portal along with a certified true copy of the Partnership Deed, partner ID/address proofs, and registered office proof (NOC + utility bill).
  4. 4. Receive Certificate of Registration: Upon verification by the Registrar, the firm's name is entered into the Register of Firms and a formal registration certificate is issued (~7–15 days).
  5. 5. Open Current Account: Present the Firm PAN, Partnership Deed, and ROF Certificate to your bank.

Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Hindu Law / Hindu Succession Act, 1956, and recognized as a separate taxable entity under the Income-tax Act.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Minimum 2 coparceners/members belonging to a Hindu, Buddhist, Jain, or Sikh family, headed by a Karta (senior-most manager), plus an HUF Deed and ancestral/gifted corpus.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹2,000–₹5,000 (for HUF Deed, rubber stamp, and HUF PAN application); ~7–15 days to obtain PAN and open an HUF bank account.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Unlimited personal liability for the Karta; limited to their share of family property for Coparceners. Holds a separate PAN card and enjoys its own basic income tax exemption slab.
  • CONTEXT: A traditional family wealth-management, real-estate holding, and tax-splitting vehicle; unsuitable for raising external venture capital.
  • EXAMPLE: A multi-generational family funding a long-term, closed-loop agricultural research project on their ancestral land to crossbreed a drought-resistant heirloom wheat strain.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Execute an HUF Deed: Draft a declaration deed on stamp paper naming the Karta (manager) and all Coparceners (family members), witnessing the creation of the HUF corpus via ancestral property, a will, or a specific gift from a non-member.
  2. 2. Engrave the HUF Rubber Stamp: Create a rectangular stamp reading "For [Name of Karta] HUF — Karta".
  3. 3. Apply for an HUF PAN Card: File Form 49A online under the "Hindu Undivided Family" category, attaching the notarized HUF Deed and the Karta's identity/address proofs.
  4. 4. Open an HUF Bank Account & Demat: Use the HUF PAN, HUF Deed, and HUF stamp to open a dedicated bank account and deposit the initial gift/ancestral seed capital.

Association of Persons (AOP) & Body of Individuals (BOI)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Income-tax Act, 2025 (erstwhile Section 2(31)(v) of the Income-tax Act, 1961).
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: 2 or more individuals (BOI) or entities/individuals (AOP) executing a joint venture or common income-producing agreement without incorporating a separate company or partnership.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹5,000–₹20,000 (contract/consortium agreement drafting + PAN application); ~5–10 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Joint and several liability among members. If member shares are indeterminate, taxed at the Maximum Marginal Rate (MMR).
  • CONTEXT: Frequently used by EPC/infrastructure engineering consortiums bidding together on government tenders; heavily scrutinized by tax authorities.
  • EXAMPLE: A temporary consortium of independent civil engineers, material scientists, and a local construction firm pooling resources to research and develop a localized, flood-resistant porous asphalt formula to bid on a specific state highway tender.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Execute the Consortium / Joint Venture Agreement: Draft and sign a binding agreement on stamp paper between the participating individuals (BOI) or corporate/individual entities (AOP), clearly specifying whether member profit-sharing ratios are determinate (fixed) or indeterminate.
  2. 2. Apply for an AOP / BOI PAN & TAN: File Form 49A selecting the AOP or BOI status category, attaching the executed agreement and authorized signatory resolution.
  3. 3. Open Escrow / Current Account & Obtain Project GST: Open a dedicated joint-signatory bank account and apply for project-specific GST registration (GST REG-01) to raise invoices for the tender or venture.

Self-Help Group (SHG) & Joint Liability Group (JLG)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: NABARD / RBI Microfinance Guidelines and NRLM / SRLM frameworks.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: 10–20 members (for SHGs) or 4–10 members (for JLGs) from similar socio-economic backgrounds pooling micro-savings.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ₹0–₹500; ~1–4 weeks (formation of group resolution and savings bank linkage).
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Mutual/peer-guaranteed joint liability for bank loans. Minimal compliance (internal ledger book).
  • CONTEXT: Grassroots rural micro-enterprise and artisanal manufacturing collectives; often upgraded into a Producer Company or Co-operative at scale.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Convene Founding Meeting & Pass Resolution: Gather 10–20 members (SHG) or 4–10 members (JLG), pass a written resolution in a bound minute book electing two to three group leaders (President, Secretary, Treasurer), and agree on regular savings rules.
  2. 2. Maintain Internal Books (Panchasutra): Record weekly/monthly attendance, savings, internal micro-loans, and repayments for ~3–6 months (or register directly with the local State Rural/Urban Livelihood Mission - SRLM/NULM).
  3. 3. Execute Bank Linkage: Submit the group resolution, member KYC proofs, and bylaws to a local commercial or cooperative bank branch to open a group savings/credit account under NABARD's SHG-Bank Linkage guideline.

Local Authority & Artificial Juridical Person (AJP)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Income-tax Act, 2025 (erstwhile Section 2(31)(vi) & (vii) of the 1961 Act) and respective municipal/university statutes.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Created by state notification, royal/legislative charter, or religious consecration (e.g., Panchayats, Municipalities, Cantonment Boards, Port Trusts, statutory Universities, Bar Councils, and consecrated Temple Deities/Idols).
  • CONTEXT: Residual statutory classifications ensuring every non-human legal entity capable of owning property or suing/being sued is captured within the Indian tax net.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Establish Statutory / Consecrated Charter: Created via state government gazette notification (for Municipalities/Panchayats), legislative charter (for Universities/Bar Councils), or formal religious consecration/endowment deed (for Temple Deities).
  2. 2. Obtain Entity PAN & Tax Exemption: File Form 49A under the "Local Authority" or "Artificial Juridical Person" category using the Gazette Notification, Charter Act, or Endowment Deed, followed by relevant Income-tax exemption filings (such as Section 10(20), 10(23C), or 12AB).

02. The Trust, Endowment & Capital Pooling Architecture

Trusts are fiduciary legal arrangements where a Settlor transfers property/capital to a Trustee to manage for Beneficiaries under a registered Trust Deed.

Public Charitable / Religious Trust (NGO)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: State-specific Public Trust Acts (e.g., Maharashtra/Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950; Gujarat Public Trusts Act) or general principles of the Indian Trusts Act, 1882.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: 1 Settlor (Author) and a minimum of 2 to 3 Trustees (no upper limit). Requires a token initial settlement corpus (typically ₹1,000–₹10,000) and a registered Trust Deed.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹5,000–₹20,000; ~7–21 days for sub-registrar deed registration (plus ~1–3 months if registering with a State Charity Commissioner and obtaining Income Tax 12AB / 80G and CSR-1 exemptions).
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Trustees carry fiduciary liability for breach of trust. Income is tax-exempt if at least 85% of annual receipts are applied to charitable objects in India and 12AB registration is maintained. Annual audit (Form 10B/10BB) costs ~₹10,000–₹30,000/year.
  • CONTEXT: Ideal for philanthropic foundations, hospitals, and schools, but irrevocable and legally unusable for commercial equity exits or dividend payouts.
  • EXAMPLE: A purely philanthropic research institute developing and open-sourcing low-cost, decentralized water purification filtration systems using locally sourced clay and moringa seeds for rural villages.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Draft the Trust Deed: Prepare the Trust Deed on non-judicial stamp paper specifying the Settlor, at least 2–3 Trustees, the initial settled property/cash corpus, charitable objects, and an irrevocability clause.
  2. 2. Register at the Sub-Registrar Office: Schedule an appointment at the local Sub-Registrar of Assurances. The Settlor, Trustees, and two witnesses appear in person with ID proofs and photographs to execute and register the deed (~7–21 days).
  3. 3. Register with the State Charity Commissioner (Where Applicable): In states with dedicated Public Trust Acts (such as Maharashtra or Gujarat), file an application with the Charity Commissioner's office to obtain a Public Trust Registration Certificate.
  4. 4. Obtain Trust PAN & Bank Account: Apply for a PAN in Form 49A (under the "Trust" category) and open a trust bank account.
  5. 5. Secure Tax & CSR Clearances: Register on NGO Darpan (NITI Aayog), file Form 10A / 10AB on the Income Tax portal for Section 12AB (tax exemption) and Section 80G (donor tax deduction), and file Form CSR-1 on the MCA portal to accept corporate CSR grants.

Waqf (Islamic Charitable / Religious Endowment)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: The Waqf Act, 1995 (as amended by the Unified Waqf Management, Empowerment, Efficiency and Development Act).
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: 1 Waqif (dedicator) permanently dedicating movable or immovable property for pious, religious, or charitable purposes, managed by a Mutawalli (manager) and registered with the State Waqf Board.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹5,000–₹15,000; ~1–3 months for Waqf Board verification and registration.
  • CONTEXT: Permanent, inalienable religious/charitable asset dedication; strictly non-commercial.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Execute the Waqfnama (Endowment Deed): The Waqif (founder) executes a formal Waqfnama on stamp paper permanently dedicating movable or immovable property for religious, pious, or charitable purposes, appointing a Mutawalli (manager) and defining the succession rules.
  2. 2. File Registration Application with the State Waqf Board: Within 3 months of creation, the Mutawalli submits an application under Section 36 to the State Waqf Board (and central UMEED portal) attaching the Waqfnama, property title deeds, revenue records, and encumbrance certificates.
  3. 3. Inquiry & Gazette Entry: The Waqf Board conducts a field survey/inquiry, verifies title and public notice, and enters the endowment into the official Register of Auqaf, issuing a Waqf Registration Certificate.

Private Trust (Family Office & Estate Engine)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: The Indian Trusts Act, 1882.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: 1 Settlor, at least 1 Trustee (corporate or individual), and specified Beneficiaries (individuals or corporate entities).
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹10,000–₹50,000+ (depending on professional structuring and state stamp duty on settled assets); ~3–10 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Specific Trusts (where beneficiary shares are fixed and known) are taxed as pass-through vehicles at the beneficiaries' rates; Discretionary Trusts (where trustees decide payouts) are taxed at the Maximum Marginal Rate (~39%–42.74%).
  • CONTEXT: Used by HNI promoters for multi-generational estate planning, ring-fencing personal wealth from business creditors, and holding promoter shares.
  • EXAMPLE: A Category-I Deep-Tech Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) pooling capital from Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNIs) to deploy patient capital into early-stage quantum computing and solid-state battery startups.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Draft the Private Trust Deed: Draft a Specific (Determinate) or Discretionary Trust Deed identifying the Settlor, Trustee(s), defined Beneficiaries, distribution triggers, and protector powers.
  2. 2. Stamp & Register the Deed: Pay applicable state stamp duty. If immovable property is settled into the trust, mandatory registration at the local Sub-Registrar of Assurances is required under Section 5 of the Indian Trusts Act; if only movable assets/cash are settled, notarization or optional registration suffices.
  3. 3. Obtain PAN & Transfer Corpus: Apply for a Trust PAN (Form 49A), open a trust bank/demat account, and transfer the initial corpus or unlisted shares into the Trustee's name held on behalf of the Trust.

03. Companies Limited by Shares (The Venture & Commercial Standard)

Governed by The Companies Act, 2013 [Section 2(22)], where shareholder liability is strictly limited to any unpaid amount on their subscribed shares.

One Person Company (OPC)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Companies Act, 2013 [Section 2(62)].
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Exactly 1 Shareholder (must be a natural person who is an Indian citizen, resident or NRI—corporate bodies are strictly barred from holding shares under Rule 3), 1 Nominee (who takes over in case of death/incapacity), and at least 1 Resident Director (stayed in India ≥ 120 days). Zero minimum paid-up capital.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹8,000–₹18,000 (via MCA SPICe+ webform); ~7–14 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Full limited liability protection. Taxed at corporate rates (~25.17% under Section 115BAA, plus dividend tax in personal hands upon extraction). Exempt from holding AGMs and requires only 1 board meeting per half-year (if >1 director). Annual compliance cost: ~₹20,000–₹40,000/year.
  • CONTEXT: Built for solo founders signing high-liability commercial contracts without co-founders or external investors. Cannot issue ESOPs or equity to VCs without converting to a Pvt Ltd.
  • EXAMPLE: A solo hardware inventor researching and stress-testing a proprietary, fire-retardant lithium-ion battery casing for electric scooters.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Single Member + Mandatory Nominee: Enter details for 1 natural-person Indian citizen Shareholder/Director and 1 natural-person Nominee (who takes over shares in the event of the sole member's death or incapacity).

Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Companies Act, 2013 [Section 2(68)].
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Minimum 2 Shareholders (maximum 200, excluding employees/ESOP holders), minimum 2 Directors (maximum 15; at least 1 must be resident in India for ≥ 120 days). Zero statutory minimum paid-up capital.

  2. 2. Register Business User Profiles on MCA V3: Create "Business User" accounts on mca.gov.in for each director/signatory and associate their DSCs using the MCA E-Signer utility.
  3. 3. Reserve Company Name (SPICe+ Part A): Propose up to 2 distinct names ending in "Private Limited" along with the NIC main business activity code. Affix DSCs of directors and certifying professional (CA/CS/CMA/Advocate), and pay the MCA stamp duty and PAN/TAN fee.

04. Limited Liability Partnerships (The Hybrid)

The statutory hybrid combining corporate limited liability with partnership operational and pass-through tax flexibility.

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: The Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Minimum 2 Partners (no upper limit; can be individuals or body corporates) and minimum 2 Designated Partners (must be individuals, at least 1 resident in India for ≥ 120 days) holding DPINs. Requires a notarized LLP Agreement filed in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. Zero minimum capital.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹6,000–₹15,000 (via MCA FiLLiP portal + stamp duty on LLP Agreement); ~7–14 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Liability is strictly limited to each partner's agreed contribution; partners are not personally liable for another partner's independent misconduct. Taxed at a flat 30% rate (plus surcharge/cess), but post-tax profit distributions to partners are 100% tax-free in the partners' hands (no dividend double-taxation). Statutory audit is mandatory only if annual turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs OR total capital contribution exceeds ₹25 Lakhs. Annual filings (Form 8 & Form 11) cost ~₹10,000–₹25,000/year.
  • CONTEXT: Ideal for consulting firms, GP Investment Manager entities, real-estate SPVs, and B2B cash-flow agencies. Poor fit for deep-tech startups because LLPs cannot issue equity shares, CCPS, or standard ESOPs to VCs and engineers.
  • EXAMPLE: A specialized metallurgical testing and failure-analysis lab providing Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) and material fatigue research as a highly paid B2B service to tier-1 automotive manufacturers.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Obtain Class-3 DSCs & MCA V3 Business User Accounts: Secure Class-3 DSCs for all proposed Designated Partners (minimum 2 individuals, at least 1 resident in India) and register their Business User profiles on mca.gov.in.
  2. 2. Reserve Name & File Incorporation via FiLLiP: Reserve the name first via RUN-LLP (₹200 fee, valid 90 days) or apply directly inside the integrated FiLLiP webform. Enter proposed name (ending in "LLP"), registered office address, NIC activity codes, partner contributions, and request allotment of up to 2 DPINs alongside automatic PAN and TAN.
  3. 3. Upload Consent & Obtain Certificate of Incorporation: Attach Form 9 (consent of Designated Partners) and subscriber sheets, affix DSCs of partners and certifying professional, and pay the MCA fee. The CRC issues the Certificate of Incorporation (Form 16) containing the LLPIN (~5–10 working days).
  4. 4. Draft, Stamp & File the LLP Agreement (Form 3 — Mandatory within 30 Days): Draft the LLP Agreement defining partner contributions, profit-sharing ratios, governance, and vesting. Print it on non-judicial stamp paper of the state-mandated value, notarize it, and file it in Form 3 on MCA V3 within 30 days of incorporation (missing this triggers a ₹100/day statutory late fee).

Section 8 Company (Corporate NGO / Research Foundation)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Companies Act, 2013 [Section 8].
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Minimum 2 Shareholders & 2 Directors (if private) or 7 Shareholders & 3 Directors (if public). Requires a central government license (Form INC-12 / SPICe+) proving objects are exclusively charitable, scientific, educational, or environmental.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹15,000–₹35,000; ~15–30 days (plus ~1–3 months for Income Tax 12AB, 80G, and MCA CSR-1 registrations).
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Limited liability. Strictly prohibits paying any dividends to members. Enjoys 100% tax exemption on surplus reinvested into its mandate (with 12AB) and highest credibility with Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) donors and government incubators. Annual compliance: ~₹30,000–₹60,000/year.
  • CONTEXT: The premier vehicle for academic research foundations, open-source consortiums, and Technology Business Incubators (TBIs), but a dead-end for commercial equity exits.
  • EXAMPLE: A university-backed applied research foundation operating a high-end nanotechnology cleanroom, dedicated to researching carbon nanotube water desalination membranes.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Reserve Name Without "Pvt Ltd / Ltd" Suffix: In SPICe+ Part A on MCA V3, select "Section 8 Company" and propose a name ending in terms such as Foundation, Institute, Association, Forum, Council, or Federation.
  2. 2. Prepare Custom e-MoA (INC-33) & Financial Projections: Inside SPICe+ Part B, draft the e-MoA (Form INC-13 / INC-33) containing mandatory Section 8 non-profit clauses (prohibiting dividends). Attach a 3-year statement of projected income and expenditure, a brief of proposed work, and declarations in Form INC-14 (CA/CS/CMA) and Form INC-15 (promoters).
  3. 3. Obtain License & Certificate of Incorporation: The CRC processes the combined application and issues the Section 8 License (Form INC-16) alongside the Certificate of Incorporation (INC-11), PAN, and TAN (~15–30 days).
  4. 4. Post-Incorporation Tax & CSR Filings: Apply for Section 12AB and Section 80G on the Income Tax portal (Form 10A/10AB), register on NGO Darpan, and file Form CSR-1 on the MCA portal.

Nidhi Company (Mutual Benefit Financial Society)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Companies Act, 2013 [Section 406] and Nidhi Rules, 2014 (as amended).
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Must be incorporated as a Public Company (minimum 7 Shareholders & 3 Directors), have a minimum paid-up equity share capital of ₹10 Lakhs, and obtain Central Government declaration in Form NDH-4 within 120 days of incorporation (requiring at least 200 members and Net Owned Funds of ₹20 Lakhs before commencing lending/deposit operations).
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹30,000–₹75,000; ~15–20 days for incorporation + up to 4–6 months for mandatory NDH-4 approval.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Limited liability, exempt from core RBI NBFC registration, but heavily policed by the MCA (high rejection rate on NDH-4 filings). Prohibited from advertising for deposits, issuing preference shares, or lending to non-members. Annual compliance: ~₹50,000–₹1,00,000/year.
  • CONTEXT: Localized member-only thrift and credit institutions.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Incorporate as a Public Company via SPICe+: File SPICe+ (INC-32) on MCA V3 with at least 7 Subscribers and 3 Directors, a name ending in "Nidhi Limited", a minimum paid-up equity share capital of ₹10 Lakhs, and an MoA object restricted exclusively to member savings/lending.
  2. 2. Meet the 120-Day Statutory Thresholds: Within 120 days of incorporation, the company must onboard at least 200 members and increase its Net Owned Funds (NOF) to at least ₹20 Lakhs.
  3. 3. File Form NDH-4 for Central Government Declaration: Within 120 days of incorporation, file Form NDH-4 on MCA V3 applying for official Central Government declaration as a Nidhi Company. (Under Rule 3A of the Nidhi Rules, a newly incorporated Nidhi cannot raise deposits from or lend to any member until the Central Government approves Form NDH-4 and issues the formal notification).

Dormant Company (The IP / Asset Vault)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Companies Act, 2013 [Section 455].
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Any newly formed or existing company holding an asset or Intellectual Property with no "significant accounting transactions" (other than statutory MCA fees, allotment of shares to fulfill the Act, and office/record maintenance payments) that applies via Form MSC-1.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹5,000–₹15,000 (for MSC-1 conversion after incorporation); ~7–15 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Retains its corporate legal shield while enjoying minimal compliance (2 board meetings/year, no cash flow statement, simplified annual return Form MSC-3). Can remain dormant for a maximum of 5 consecutive years before it must either reactivate (MSC-4) or be struck off. Annual maintenance: ~₹10,000–₹20,000/year.
  • CONTEXT: Useful for parking patents, land, or brand trademarks for a future project while pausing operational burn.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Incorporate a Standard Company: First incorporate a standard Private or Public Limited Company via SPICe+ (INC-32) to hold the patent, trademark, or land asset.
  2. 2. Pass Special Resolution: Convene a General Meeting (or obtain consent from shareholders holding at least 75% in value) authorizing the company to apply for Dormant status, and file the resolution in Form MGT-14 within 30 days.
  3. 3. File Form MSC-1 on MCA V3: Submit Form MSC-1 along with a Chartered Accountant's certificate confirming no significant accounting transactions, no outstanding public deposits, no disputed tax/labor dues, and written consent from any lenders.
  4. 4. Receive Certificate of Dormant Status: The Registrar issues the Certificate in Form MSC-2, placing the company on the Register of Dormant Companies (maintained via simplified annual Form MSC-3 filings for up to 5 years).

Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) & Core Investment Company (CIC)

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Act, 1934 [Section 45-IA] + Companies Act, 2013.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Must be a registered Company. Triggered statutorily under the RBI "50-50 Test" when >50% of a company's total assets are financial assets (shares/loans) AND >50% of its gross income comes from those financial assets. Requires a minimum Net Owned Fund (NOF) of ₹10 Crore for standard Base-Layer lending/investment NBFCs (₹2 Crore for pure P2P/Account Aggregator niches). Unregistered CICs (holding group shares with assets <₹100 Crore and not raising public funds) are exempt from RBI registration; Systemically Important CICs (CIC-ND-SI, assets ≥ ₹100 Crore with public funds) require RBI registration.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹3 Lakhs–₹15 Lakhs+ in legal/advisory costs; ~4–9 months for RBI Certificate of Registration (CoR).
  • CONTEXT: Critical watch-out for Holding Companies (Parent X)—if your HoldCo holds startup shares and earns mostly dividends/capital gains without >50% active operating/studio revenue, it risks being classified as an unregistered NBFC.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Incorporate Company & Infuse Net Owned Fund (NOF): Incorporate a Private or Public Limited Company via SPICe+ (INC-32) with financial/investment objects in the MoA, and deposit the mandatory minimum Net Owned Fund (₹10 Crore for standard Base-Layer Investment & Credit NBFCs; ₹100 Crore+ asset threshold with public funds for a registered CIC-ND-SI) into a Fixed Deposit free from any liens in a scheduled commercial bank.
  2. 2. File Online on the RBI Pravaah Portal: Log in to the RBI Pravaah Portal (pravaah.rbi.org.in), complete the NBFC/CIC registration application, and upload the Statutory Auditor's NOF Certificate, CIBIL credit reports of all directors/shareholders, Banker's Confidential Report, Fit & Proper declarations, and a 5-year business plan.
  3. 3. Receive RBI Certificate of Registration (CoR): Upon background verification and due diligence by the RBI Department of Regulation (~4–9 months), the RBI issues the Certificate of Registration under Section 45-IA.

06. Corporate Group & Statutory Relational Classifications

These are statutory statuses applied on top of standard companies or LLPs that drastically alter tax, audit, and investment privileges:

Holding Company [Sec 2(46)] & Subsidiary Company [Sec 2(87)]

  • STATUTORY RULE: Company X becomes a Holding Company of Company A if X controls the composition of A's Board of Directors OR exercises/controls more than 50% of the total voting power of A. Under Section 186(1), Indian companies are barred from making investments through more than 2 layers of investment subsidiaries.
  • COMPLIANCE IMPACT: Both the Holding Company and the Subsidiary automatically lose "Small Company" exemptions under Section 2(85) and must prepare Consolidated Financial Statements (CFS).
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Via Fresh Incorporation (Top-Down): Incorporate the Subsidiary or Joint Venture Company via SPICe+ (INC-32) on MCA V3. In subscriber details of SPICe+ Part B and e-MoA, list the Holding Company (Parent X) as corporate subscriber holding >50% of equity shares (for a Subsidiary, along with 1 nominee shareholder owning 1 share for the 2-member rule) or 20%–50% voting power (for an Associate/JV), attaching a certified Board Resolution under Section 179(3)(e) / Section 186 authorizing Parent X's representative to sign.
  2. 2. Via Share Swap / Acquisition (Bottom-Up Roll-Up): Incorporate Parent X via SPICe+, pass Board and Shareholder resolutions under Section 186 and Section 62(1)(c), execute stamped Share Transfer Deeds (Form SH-4) transferring founders' shares in the existing company to Parent X in exchange for fresh shares allotted by Parent X (filed via Form PAS-3), and update the Register of Members (Form MGT-1).

DPIIT-Recognized "Startup" Entity

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: DPIIT Notification G.S.R. 127(E) (and expanded Deep-Tech Startup norms) + Section 80-IAC of the Income-tax Act.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Must be incorporated solely as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or LLP. Eligible for up to 10 years from incorporation with turnover under ₹100 Crore (expanded up to 20 years and ₹300 Crore turnover for recognized Deep-Tech R&D startups).
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ₹0 government fee via the National Single Window System (NSWS) / Startup India portal; ~2–7 days for DPIIT recognition (~2–6 months if applying to the Inter-Ministerial Board for the Section 80-IAC 3-year 100% income-tax holiday).
  • CONTEXT: Unlocks 80% patent fee rebates, Form 18A fast-track patent examination, GeM tender turnover/EMD exemptions, SISFS/NIDHI grants, and deferred ESOP taxation.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Incorporate Qualifying Entity: First register a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership Firm.
  2. 2. Register on the National Single Window System (NSWS): Log in to nsws.gov.in (linked with startupindia.gov.in), add the "Registration as a Startup" approval to your dashboard, auto-verify CIN/LLPIN and PAN, upload the Certificate of Incorporation, pitch deck/prototype video, patent/IP details (if any), and self-certify compliance.

08. Societies & Co-operatives

Democratic and membership-governed vehicles for academic associations, scientific research bodies, and mass producer collectives.

Registered Society

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Societies Registration Act, 1860 (alongside state-specific acts like the Karnataka or Maharashtra Societies Registration Acts).
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Minimum 7 Founding Members (for a state-level society) or 8 Founding Members from at least 8 different Indian states (for an All-India National Society). Managed by an elected Governing Body / Executive Committee.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹5,000–₹20,000; ~15–45 days with the State Registrar of Societies.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Annual list of governing body members and audited accounts must be filed with the Registrar (~₹10,000–₹25,000/year).
  • CONTEXT: Widely used for academic/scientific associations, professional bodies, R&D autonomous institutes, and residential maintenance associations.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Convene Founding General Body & Draft Memorandum/Rules: Gather at least 7 Founding Members (state society) or 8 Members from 8 different states (All-India society). Pass a resolution adopting Society name, Memorandum of Association (objects & elected Governing Body), and Rules & Regulations (Bylaws).
  2. 2. Submit Application to the State Registrar of Societies: File the signed Memorandum, Rules & Regulations, President's affidavit on stamp paper, member ID proofs, and registered office NOC/utility bill on your state's Registrar of Societies portal (or physically at the District Registrar's office) along with statutory registration fee (~₹50 to ₹2,000).
  3. 3. Receive Society Registration Certificate: Upon scrutiny, the Registrar issues the Certificate of Registration under the Act (~15–45 days), after which the Society obtains its PAN (Form 49A) and opens its bank account.

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: State Co-operative Societies Acts (for single-state operations) or the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 (for multi-state operations).

09. Foreign Entry & Offshore-Onshore Vehicles (FEMA / RBI / SEBI / IFSCA Regulated)

For foreign corporations and global funds establishing a presence or deploying capital in India:

Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS) / Foreign Subsidiary

  • GOVERNING STATUTE: Companies Act, 2013 + FEMA (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules, 2019.
  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Standard Indian Pvt Ltd (min. 2 Shareholders, 2 Directors including 1 Indian Resident Director) where 100% (or >50%) of equity is held by the foreign parent (typically via 99.99% parent + 0.01% nominee shareholder to satisfy the 2-member rule). Requires apostilled/legalized foreign parent board resolutions and Form FC-GPR reporting to the RBI via the FIRMS portal upon share allotment.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹35,000–₹1,00,000 (including apostille, FEMA FC-GPR filings, and legal fees); ~15–30 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: Treated as an Indian domestic company for tax (~25.17%, vs. 35%+ for a foreign branch), ring-fences the foreign parent from Indian liabilities, and requires annual FLA (Foreign Liabilities and Assets) return filing with the RBI (~₹50,000–₹1,20,000/year). (Note: Excludes Small Company exemptions).
  • CONTEXT: The cleanest, most tax-efficient vehicle for foreign parents building R&D labs, engineering centers (GCCs), or commercial operations in India.
PROCEDURE:
  1. 1. Apostille / Legalize Foreign Parent Documents: Pass a Board Resolution of the Foreign Parent Company authorizing incorporation of the Indian WOS and naming authorized representative plus nominee shareholder (e.g., 99.99% Foreign Parent + 0.01% Nominee). Have Certificate of Incorporation, Charter/Bylaws, Board Resolution, and foreign directors' Passports/Address Proofs notarized and Apostilled (under Hague Convention) or consularized by the Indian Embassy in home country.
  2. 2. File SPICe+ (INC-32) on MCA V3: Onboard at least 1 Indian Resident Director (with PAN and Class-3 DSC), reserve company name in SPICe+ Part A, attach Apostilled foreign subscriber documents in SPICe+ Part B, and receive the Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and TAN (~15–25 days).
  3. 3. Inward FDI Remittance (SWIFT + FIRC/KYC): Open the Indian WOS current bank account with an Authorized Dealer (AD Category-I) Bank. Wire subscribed share capital from Foreign Parent overseas bank account via SWIFT (purpose code P0006 — foreign direct investment towards equity) and obtain Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) and KYC Report from AD Bank.
  4. 4. File Form FC-GPR on the RBI FIRMS Portal & INC-20A: Create an Entity Master and Business User account on the RBI FIRMS Portal (firms.rbi.org.in), allot shares within 60 days of funds, file Form FC-GPR (with CS certificate and Valuation Certificate) within 30 days of share allotment, and file Form INC-20A on MCA V3 within 180 days.

  • MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Foreign parent must have a profit-making track record during the immediately preceding 5 financial years in its home country and a Net Worth of at least USD 100,000 for a BO (3 years profit & $50,000 net worth for LO; or secured executed project contract for PO). Requires Form FNC approval via an Authorized Dealer (AD Category-I) Bank and registration as a Foreign Company (Form FC-1) with the MCA within 30 days.
  • SETUP COST & TIMELINE: ~₹50,000–₹1,50,000; ~30–60 days.
  • LIABILITY & TAX / COMPLIANCE: No separate legal identity (foreign parent has 100% unlimited liability for Indian operations). BO permitted to conduct export/import, consultancy, R&D, and software development (barred from retail/manufacturing outside SEZs). Taxed as Foreign Company at 35% base rate (~38.22% effective).
